About SENO Birds Test Lab

SENO Birds Test Lab is a biotechnology laboratory focused on avian DNA testing services for breeders, aviary operators, and bird organizations.

Laboratory Focus

Our team provides DNA identification, bird species identification, avian genetic analysis, and breeding verification services with standardized reporting.

Species Coverage

We support parrots, cockatiels, macaws, budgies, lovebirds, finches, canaries, pigeons, doves, and other companion birds.

Quality Framework

All sample analysis workflows use documented SOPs, chain-of-custody practices, and quality control checkpoints.
