About SENO Birds Test Lab

SENO Birds Test Lab is a biotechnology laboratory focused on bird DNA testing services for breeders, aviary operators, bird clubs, and research-support organizations. Our goal is to deliver clear DNA identification outputs that help partners improve record integrity and breeding program planning.

Laboratory Mission and Service Philosophy

We use neutral scientific language and standardized workflows. Services are designed for identification, species documentation, and breeding verification support—not medical diagnosis or treatment.

Bird Species Coverage Across Companion and Aviary Segments

Our workflows are designed for parrots, cockatiels, macaws, budgies, lovebirds, finches, canaries, pigeons, doves, and exotic companion birds. This broad species coverage supports both pet-oriented and performance-oriented programs.

Operational Quality Framework

Each submission follows a chain-of-custody process: sample labeling, accession logging, marker testing, quality checks, and report release. This approach improves consistency in multi-batch and multi-species testing.
